The assignment brings the lessons together. Learners hand in their work on the published site, and you grade it with the rubric.

```assignment
id: final
title: Final assignment
instructions: |
  Describe what to make, how long it should be and in what form to hand it in.
due: ""
rubric:
  - criterion: Understands the central concept
    points: 4
  - criterion: Applies the method correctly
    points: 4
  - criterion: Writes clearly
    points: 2
files: true
```
